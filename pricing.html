COMMERCIAL TERMS

Pricing & Billing FAQs

Clear terms, zero hidden line-items, and transparent billing schedules.

Are there any setup or onboarding fees?

Zero. Unlike legacy providers that charge thousands in "implementation fees" or charge per state tax registration, Stackly includes complete white-glove onboarding and multi-state nexus registrations in your base subscription.

What about year-end W-2/1099 filing surcharges?

None. We never charge extra for year-end filings. Legacy providers often charge $3 to $5 per W-2 or 1099 form, but all statutory reporting and filing is 100% included in your standard plan at no additional cost.

How do contractor (1099) payouts compare?

Contractors are billed dynamically only during the months they receive an active disbursement. If a contractor is inactive for a given month, you are charged $0.00 for their profile on the platform.

Can we switch to Annual billing?

Yes. You can switch to annual billing at any point to unlock the 20% institutional discount. Annual treasury billing also provides access to dedicated yield rebates on tax impound balances.
